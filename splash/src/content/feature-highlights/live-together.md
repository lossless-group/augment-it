Research is rarely a solo job. When a teammate adds a source or spins up a new
strategy, everyone else watching that workspace sees it appear right away —
you're always looking at the same shared picture, not a stale copy you have to
reconcile later. Every change is signed with who made it, so the trail of who
added what is never in question.
