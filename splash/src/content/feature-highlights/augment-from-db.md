Most research tools make you start from a file. Augment It now lets you start
from what you already know: pick an organization, and one screen shows its
website and social profiles, the blogs and newsrooms it publishes through, the
source materials you've collected, and the people connected to it.

Everything on that screen grows in place. Paste a link and it files itself
under the right heading. Click the search icon next to any list and a search
panel opens beside the card — the search phrase stays editable, because you
often know a better way to word it — and any result you like joins the list in
one click. Add a person to the organization and their affiliation is recorded
automatically, roles and all.

Blogs get one more trick: press **scan** on any publication feed and Augment It
reads it fresh, marks what you've already collected, and lets you pull in just
the new pieces. Keeping up with an organization's output becomes a
thirty-second habit instead of an afternoon.
