You used to fire one search and wait, watching a single column until it
finished. Now every search you kick off drops into a queue on the right as its
own card — showing what it's working on and roughly how long it usually takes.
Start three, start ten, then go do something else. When results arrive the card
signals you; open it, keep what's good, and clear it. Fire many, deal with them
as they come.
