Hand it a thin spreadsheet and watch it get smarter. For each person, Augment It
goes and does the legwork you'd normally do by hand — finding their role, their
organization, and the details that matter — and lays it out for you to confirm or
tweak. You're never handing the wheel to a robot; you're skipping the boring part.
